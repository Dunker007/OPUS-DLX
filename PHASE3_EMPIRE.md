## 📋 PHASE 3 DELIVERABLES (90 Minutes)

### **🔍 INTELLIGENCE LAYER** (20 minutes)

#### 1. **Multi-Platform Opportunity Aggregator** (`Engines/Intelligence/opportunity-aggregator.ps1`)
**Purpose:** Scan ALL major platforms simultaneously for problems worth solving

**Data Sources:**
- Reddit API - Find complaints, pain points, feature requests
- Twitter/X API - Trending topics, viral complaints
- GitHub Issues - Missing tools, library gaps
- ProductHunt - Product gaps, comment analysis
- Hacker News - Technical discussions, needs
- Indie Hackers - Revenue models, successful patterns
- LinkedIn - B2B opportunities, corporate pain points

**Output:** Scored opportunities with:
- Demand score (1-10)
- Competition score (1-10)
- Monetization potential ($)
- Implementation difficulty (1-10)
- Time to market (days)
- Recommended price point
- Target audience profile

#### 2. **Market Research Agent** (`Engines/Intelligence/market-researcher.ps1`)
**Purpose:** Deep-dive validation using AI

**Process:**
1. Take top opportunity from aggregator
2. Research competing solutions (pricing, features, reviews)
3. Analyze target audience (subreddits, forums, demographics)
4. Estimate TAM (Total Addressable Market)
5. Project revenue (conservative, moderate, aggressive)
6. Generate go-to-market strategy
7. Create positioning statement
8. Output: 10-page market research report

#### 3. **Competitor Analyzer** (`Engines/Intelligence/competitor-analyzer.ps1`)
**Purpose:** Automated competitive intelligence

**Features:**
- Scrape competitor websites (pricing, features, testimonials)
- Monitor competitor social media mentions
- Track their blog post frequency and topics
- Analyze their SEO keywords
- Estimate their traffic (SimilarWeb API)
- Identify their weaknesses (review analysis)
- Generate "How we're better" positioning
